# Solstice — design direction

The house is the interface's central image. A single uninterrupted camera move explains the transition from poolside to home; scroll gives the visitor control. No automatic camera playback or scroll hijacking.

Palette: Obsidian #141715; Pearl #F5F5F1; Smoke #B8BFBA; Brass #C8B18A; Glass rgba(20,23,21,.28). Manrope variable is self-hosted: light, tightly spaced display; regular reading copy; medium navigation and numbered captions.

Layout: floating masthead / full-bleed photographic canvas / lower-left editorial chapter / lower-right chapter rail / quiet progress line. The lower shadow supports text without tinting the architecture. Generous empty space preserves the facade. A compact appointment panel follows the last chapter.

Principles: image first; one motion narrative; no ornamental cards; tangible progress; keyboard and reduced-motion alternatives. Preload all compressed frames, retain a bounded decoded frame window, and prioritize the exact requested frame after fast scroll jumps.
