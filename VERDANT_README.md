# Verdant Courtyard Residence

A furnished modern villa concept created in Blender, with a detached pool, pergola lounge, and landscaped grounds.

- House: 18 × 12 m footprint, two bedrooms, shared bathroom, living and dining spaces, fitted kitchen.
- Scale: metres; 3.2 m walls; nominal 1.5 m corridor and 1 m internal door openings.
- Pool: 9 × 4 m with submerged steps, ladder, rippled water, mosaic lining and stone coping.
- Garden: 14 branching trees, evergreen borders, agaves, terracotta planters and path lights.
- Materials: portable procedural oak grain, travertine, plaster, woven fabrics, stone, foliage, water and glass.

Open `Verdant_Courtyard_Residence.blend` and select one of the four named cameras in collection **11 Cameras**. Hide collection **02 Roof · hide for interior** for a furnished floor plan overview. Keep the roof visible for interior renders.

This is an architectural visualization concept. It is not a construction or permit set. A site-specific design needs local checks for structure, setbacks, drainage, accessibility and pool safety.
